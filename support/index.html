支持与常见问题

第一次打开要等多久？

AI 模型约 3.7 GB。iOS 26 及以后，模型随 App 一起从 App Store 下载，装好就能直接用；更早的系统第一次打开时要下载，请连接 Wi-Fi，并让 App 停在前台，通常几分钟就好。下载完成后，App 不需要联网。

哪些手机能用？

需要 6 GB 以上内存的 iPhone（iPhone 13 Pro、iPhone 14 或更新的机型）。内存更小的手机会显示"内存不够"，因为模型在那上面跑不动。

试用和购买

免费下载后可以先免费试用 3 天，不限次数。之后一次性购买就能一直用下去，不是订阅，不会自动扣费。换了手机或重装 App，在设置里点"恢复购买"即可。

我的对话会被上传吗？

不会。所有对话都在你的手机上处理，不会发送给我们或任何人。详见隐私政策。

如果你现在很难受

如果你有伤害自己的念头，请马上联系身边信任的人、当地的求助热线或急救电话。本应用是灵修辅助，不能代替牧者、医生或心理咨询师。

Support & FAQ

How long does the first launch take?

The AI models take about 3.7 GB. On iOS 26 and later they download from the App Store together with the app, so it is ready to use once installed. On earlier versions the first launch downloads them; please use Wi-Fi and keep the app open, it usually takes a few minutes. After that the app works without an internet connection.

Which iPhones are supported?

iPhones with 6 GB of memory or more (iPhone 13 Pro, iPhone 14 or newer). On iPhones with less memory the app shows a "not enough memory" screen, because the model cannot run there.

Free trial and purchase

The app is free to download and includes a 3-day free trial with unlimited use. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks it for good. No subscription, no automatic charges. On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, tap "Restore purchases" in Settings.

Are my conversations uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your iPhone and never sent to us or anyone else. See the privacy policy.

If you are struggling right now

If you are having thoughts of harming yourself, please contact someone you trust, a local crisis line or emergency services right away. Bible Convo is a devotional companion, not a substitute for a pastor, doctor or counselor.
