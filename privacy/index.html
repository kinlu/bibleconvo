经文对话 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日。

经文对话（下称"本应用"）在你的 iPhone 上运行，不收集你的任何个人信息。

我们不收集的信息

  • 对话内容：你输入的话和本应用的回答，都由手机上的 AI 模型处理，不会发送给我们或任何第三方。对话记录只保存在这台 iPhone 上：不进 iCloud 或电脑备份，手机锁屏时是加密的。你可以在历史对话里随时删除某一段或全部删除，删除本应用时也会一并删除。
  • 设置：界面语言、外观、经文译本、字号等设置只保存在你的手机上。
  • 账号、联系方式、位置、通讯录、照片：本应用没有账号，也不读取这些信息。
  • 使用数据和诊断信息：本应用不包含任何分析、广告或崩溃上报工具，不追踪你。

会联网的情况

  1. 下载模型：本应用要用到两个 AI 模型文件，约 3.7 GB。在 iOS 26 及以后的系统上，它们随 App 一起从 App Store 下载；在更早的系统上，或 App Store 的下载不可用时，本应用会在第一次使用时从 Hugging Face（huggingface.co）下载。和下载任何文件一样，下载服务器能看到你的 IP 地址，但请求里不包含你的对话或任何个人信息。下载完成后，本应用不需要联网。
  2. 购买：免费试用和购买由 Apple 的 App Store 处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。我们看不到你的 Apple 账号或付款信息。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

安全提示

本应用是灵修辅助，不能代替牧者、医生或心理咨询师。如果你有伤害自己的念头，请马上联系身边信任的人、当地的求助热线或急救电话。

变更

本政策如有变更，会在这个页面更新，并注明生效日期。

兒童

本應用不以 13 歲以下的兒童為對象，也不收集任何人的個人資料。

Bible Convo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026.

Bible Convo ("the app") runs on your iPhone and does not collect any personal information.

What we do not collect

  • Your conversations. What you type and the app's replies are processed by an AI model on your iPhone and are never sent to us or to anyone else. Your conversation history is stored only on this iPhone: it is left out of iCloud and computer backups, and it is encrypted while the phone is locked. You can delete one conversation or all of them from the history at any time, and deleting the app deletes them too.
  • Your settings. Language, appearance, Bible translation and text size are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Accounts, contact details, location, contacts or photos. The app has no accounts and does not access this information.
  • Usage data and diagnostics. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools and does not track you.

When the app uses the network

  1. Downloading the models. The app needs two AI model files (about 3.7 GB). On iOS 26 and later they are downloaded from the App Store together with the app. On earlier versions, or if the App Store download is unavailable, the app downloads them from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) on first use. As with any download, the server can see your IP address, but the requests contain none of your conversations or personal information. After the download, the app works without a network connection.
  2. Purchases. The free trial and the purchase are handled by Apple's App Store under Apple's privacy policy. We do not see your Apple Account or payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Safety

The app is a devotional companion, not a substitute for a pastor, doctor or counselor. If you are having thoughts of harming yourself, please contact someone you trust, a local crisis line or emergency services right away.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with its effective date.
